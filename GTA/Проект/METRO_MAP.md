# Portside Metro

The open world is a 960 × 960 regional map built around five distinct urban zones while preserving the original Portside center and mission locations.

- **PORTSIDE** — original downtown and courier mission hub.
- **VICE BEACH** — eastern beachfront high-rises, marina and nightlife landmarks.
- **WEST HARBOR** — western industrial port, warehouses, freight and shipyard blocks.
- **NORTHSIDE** — northern residential/civic city with lower-rise neighborhoods.
- **SUNPORT** — southern airport and cargo district.

Regional roads at ±320 and ±400 connect the satellite cities to the original avenues. The enlarged world keeps the existing vehicle, pedestrian, police, collision and mission systems rather than introducing a separate map mode.
